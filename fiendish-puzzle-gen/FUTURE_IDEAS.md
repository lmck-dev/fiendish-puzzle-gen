# Fiendish Puzzle Gen — Future Ideas & Roadmap

## Language Translator / Dictionary Engine Enhancements
- **Automated Dictionary Extraction Tool:** Build a parser or scraping script that can ingest a structured PDF, HTML file, or raw text body of a community wiki dictionary. It would use regex or a simple NLP heuristic to identify `English_Word` -> `Translated_Word` pairs and automatically bulk-import them into a custom language.
- **Web-based Dictionary Fetcher:** Potentially add an integration to just point a URL to a known layout (like Eldamo, Thuum.org, or Klingon Wiki) and crawl/grab the vocab.

## Phase 3 Features (Coming Up)
- **Crossword Generator:** Auto-generating interlocking crossword grids from clue/answer pairs, with optional clue cyphering.
- **Anagram Generator:** Scrambling words with adjustable difficulty and optional hints. Can be chained with cyphers or translators.
- **Maze Generator:** Printable mazes with hidden paths that spell out words or messages along the correct route.

## Meta Features
- **Puzzle Chainer:** Output of one puzzle becomes the key for the next.
- **Red Herring Injector:** Auto-generates plausible but fake clues into crosswords or word searches.
